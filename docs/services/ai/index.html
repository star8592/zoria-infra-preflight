05 / Frequently asked questions

Know the boundaries.

Does this include custom AI model training?

No. Scope covers a single specified application or workflow. Training and new feature development require separate scoping.

Can you guarantee transcript or prediction accuracy?

No. Accuracy depends on model and data quality; acceptance is based on the agreed technical smoke test.

Are cloud bills and paid licenses included?

No. Customer-provided hosting and third-party fees are separate.

Can you use copyrighted third-party datasets?

Only when the customer demonstrates applicable lawful permission; unauthorized assets are excluded.

05 / Frequently asked questions

Know the boundaries.

Can you guarantee a particular speedup?

No. Environment repairs and performance gains depend on hardware and workload. Measurements, when included, follow an agreed method.

Are H100 or other GPU rental charges included?

No. The customer supplies and pays for needed test hardware or cloud GPU time.

Do you train custom models?

No. This service addresses runtime compatibility, diagnostics and infrastructure configuration, not model training.

Can you change drivers on production?

Only after an explicit compatibility check, rollback plan and approved change window.
